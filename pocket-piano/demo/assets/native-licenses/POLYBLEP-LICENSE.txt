RetroGameSynth.java is an ALTERED Java adaptation of the small BLEP/BLAMP
edge-correction primitives and pulse/triangle/saw equations. Martin Finke /
Tale are the original authors of the upstream implementation. Patch routing,
envelope, PWM gate, sample-hold arpeggio, FM, sync and chorus coefficients in
this project's adaptation are original. It is not the unmodified upstream.
