This application includes 30 samples at minor-third intervals, A0–C8.
Changes: MP3 sources decoded to 48 kHz, 16-bit, mono PCM WAV; encoder-leading
silence removed while preserving the attack; tails limited to 8 seconds with a
20 ms fade at the end. Playback transposes nearby samples and applies velocity,
attack, release, voice mixing and volume. This modified subset is attributed
to Alexander Holm; no endorsement by the author is implied.

The original instrument description documents 16 velocity layers; this app's
Tone.js subset contains one sample per sampled key and scales velocity gain.
It does not claim the full original velocity/resonance/pedal sample library.
