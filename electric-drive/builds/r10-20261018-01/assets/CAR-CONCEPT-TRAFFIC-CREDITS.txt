Changes made for 极限电驱 R4:
- Removed cabin/interior and under-hood detail, license/emblem meshes.
- Corrected the source front-wheel showcase steering before distance rendering.
- Applied 13 percent mesh collapse LOD, retaining 21,929 triangles in total.
- Replaced materials with six opaque PBR materials without texture dependencies.
- Retained four wheel pivots for animated wheels; runtime meshes are instanced.
- Normal traffic uses varied paint colors; the rear pursuer uses red paint.
