SkyMyth instrumental fantasy music sources
=========================================

Both tracks were published by the named authors under CC0 1.0 Universal.
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Optional attribution is retained below; no author endorsement is implied.

The runtime pair replaces the previously rejected nautical/vocal music.
Source loop length, melody, pitch and tempo are preserved. Runtime files are
44.1 kHz stereo Ogg Vorbis, with constant gain to approximately -18 LUFS.
An 8 ms edge ramp prevents a waveform discontinuity at each loop boundary.
Gain, edge ramp and codec conversion are local adaptations for game mixing.
