Usage in SkyMyth:

- Source Han Serif CN Heavy: large Signature-style card names.
- Source Han Sans CN Bold: compact collection, hand and battlefield card names.
- Barlow Condensed Black: mana, attack and health values.

No Hearthstone or Blizzard font binary, frame, emblem or logo is included.
